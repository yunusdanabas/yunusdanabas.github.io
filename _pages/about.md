I build robotics and control systems that connect modeling, embedded sensing, ROS-based software, and real hardware. I graduated **1st in Mechatronics (2025)** from Sabancı University and completed a second B.Sc. in **Electronics (2026)**.

My research experience spans the [**PRISMA Lab**](https://prisma.dieti.unina.it/) (University of Naples Federico II), [**TUM MIRMI**](https://www.mirmi.tum.de/), and Sabancı's [**Human–Machine Interaction Laboratory**](https://hmi.sabanciuniv.edu/), working on simulation-driven development, sensing, and control.

In industry, I led technical development of **EVKA Wireless / EVKA 2.0** at [**AS Robotics (COMAU Turkey)**](https://www.asmetal.com.tr/en/products/robotics-and-automation/) — a TÜBİTAK-backed wireless tele-imitation system connecting embedded sensing, ROS 2, and COMAU robot programming — following internships at [**TÜBİTAK BİLGEM**](https://bilgem.tubitak.gov.tr/) and [**Pubinno Inc.**](https://pubinno.com/) and leading the [**SURover student robotics team**](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sabancirover/).

At Sabancı I also served as a teaching assistant for robotics, mechanisms, and motion-control courses, and built the open-source [**Dobot Robotics Lab**](/projects/dobot_robotics_lab/), a simulation-first teaching package with four progressive labs.

Selected systems, implementation details, and results are in [Projects](/projects/); the full history is on my [CV](/cv/).

## Focus areas

- **Robot control & learning** — model-based and learning-based methods for dynamic, contact-rich systems
- **Teleoperation & robot teaching** — interfaces that make industrial and mobile robots easier to command
- **Sim-to-real development** — reproducible pipelines from simulation to embedded sensing and physical hardware
